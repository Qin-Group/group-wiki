The decadal report from the US National Academy of Science on [Biological Physics](https://www.nationalacademies.org/our-work/biological-physicsphysics-of-living-systems-a-decadal-survey). Biological Physics provides a very comprehensive overview of the field. 




## Some classic readings
An early essay on the interface between physics and life
- N. Bohr. Light and life. Nature, 131(3309):457–459, 1933

Two assays by Delbruck, one of the most influential biophysicsts of the last century.
- M. Delbruck. A physicist looks at biology. Trans Conn Acad Arts Sci, 38:173, 1948
- M. Delbrück. A physicist’s renewed look at biology: twenty years later. Science, 168(3937):1312–1315,1970.

Remond Goldstein from Cambridge University has accrued a list of [classic papers](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/gold/old_literature.html) in biological physics and nonlinear dynamics.
